Claude Companion for World of Warcraft Forever
===============================================

Install (once)
  Windows: unzip this file (right-click > Extract All), then double-click "Install Claude Companion".
           If Windows asks whether to run it, choose Run (or More info > Run anyway).
  Mac:     double-click "Install Claude Companion". The first time, macOS says it can't check the
           app: click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
           Open Anyway next to "Install Claude Companion" (on older macOS: Control-click > Open).

The installer finds your WoW Forever folder (or asks you to pick it), puts the addon in
_forever_/Interface/AddOns/ClaudeCompanion and opens the app in your browser. From then on the app
starts by itself when you sign in to your computer and updates itself. Nothing else to download.

In game: type /reload if the game was running, then /cc sync. The app shows your character a few
seconds later. Open your bank, profession windows, a flight master and the auction house once
(press "Claude scan"), switch to your other spec once, then /cc sync again to fill everything in.

Your data
  Everything the addon records stays in your game's own saved file. The app syncs your history
  between your own computers through the Companion's sync service, under a private random key made
  on your computer. To use a second computer: System tab > Link another computer.

Remove
  Windows: run "Claude Companion.bat uninstall" in AddOns\ClaudeCompanion\web, then delete the
           AddOns\ClaudeCompanion folder.
  Mac:     in Terminal, /usr/bin/python3 "<AddOns>/ClaudeCompanion/web/server.py" --uninstall,
           then delete the AddOns/ClaudeCompanion folder.
